ABDL Space copies and adapts selected PIN domain/repository models, Security/PIN
setup/change/disable/lock screens, authentication tracking, biometric key/prompt
structure, and locale strings under GPL-3.0-only. Dependency injection, navigation,
themes, lifecycle integration, storage, and safety/compatibility fixes are adapted
for ABDL Space. The PIN storage uses an ABDL-specific AndroidKeyStore AES/GCM adapter.

No 2FAS screenshots, artwork, branding, BUSL/develop-branch code, or legacy
adorsys AAR is included.
